# STRATEGY — Current Business Strategy

## Strategic Thesis

The fastest path to a $1B company for a technical Solopreneur is to:

1. **Build a personal AI workforce** that can research, code, analyze, and execute at scale.
2. **Convert learning into reusable assets** — agents, templates, APIs, products, and knowledge systems.
3. **Validate commercial opportunities rapidly** through experiments rather than planning.
4. **Create compounding infrastructure** that makes each subsequent product faster and cheaper to build.
5. **Develop durable moats** through proprietary data, workflows, integrations, and domain expertise.

## Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1: AI Agent Factory
Build a system for rapidly designing, deploying, and improving autonomous agents. The goal is not just individual agents, but a factory that produces agents.

### Pillar 2: Knowledge & Learning Engine
Capture every experiment, lesson, customer insight, and reusable component in a structured, searchable system (Obsidian vault + GitHub + Hermes memories).

### Pillar 3: Commercial Validation Loop
Run fast, low-cost experiments to test B2B AI use cases. Kill weak ideas quickly. Scale winners rapidly.

### Pillar 4: Founder Leverage Multiplier
Continuously move work from the founder to agents, then from agents to full automation. Maximize leverage per founder hour.

### Pillar 5: Defensibility Through Data & Integration
Build proprietary datasets, deep integrations, and domain-specific AI systems that become harder to replicate over time.

## Current Strategic Bets
